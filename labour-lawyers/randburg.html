Frequently Asked Questions

How close is your office to Randburg?

Our office is at Prism Business Park in Fourways, a short drive from Randburg. We are happy to meet in person or by phone or video call.

Do you act for employees as well as employers in Randburg?

Yes. We act for both employers and employees. Tell us about your matter and we will explain your options.

Which CCMA office handles disputes from Randburg?

Disputes are referred to the CCMA office, or the bargaining council, with jurisdiction over the workplace and sector. We will confirm the right forum for your matter and handle the referral within the time limits.
