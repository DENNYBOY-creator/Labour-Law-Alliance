Employment Contracts & HR Policies

Employment contracts, HR policies and workplace procedures that give your business strong foundations.

Book a Consultation Call 011 219 5025

Section 29 of the BCEA requires employers to give employees written particulars of employment when they start work. A well-drafted contract and clear workplace policies prevent misunderstandings, protect the business, and make it much easier to manage discipline, performance and change fairly.

Labour Law Alliance drafts and reviews contracts and policies that fit South African law and the way your business actually works.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a verbal employment contract valid?

Yes, a verbal employment agreement is binding. However, the BCEA requires employers to give employees written particulars of employment, and a written contract greatly reduces the risk of disputes.

Are restraint of trade clauses enforceable?

Generally yes, if the restraint is reasonable: it must protect a legitimate business interest, such as confidential information or customer connections, and be reasonable in duration and area. Overly broad restraints may not be enforced.

Can we change an employee’s contract?

Terms and conditions of employment generally cannot be changed unilaterally. Changes should be consulted on and agreed with the employee or their representatives.
