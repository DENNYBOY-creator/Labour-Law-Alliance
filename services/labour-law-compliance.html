Labour Law & Compliance

Stay on the right side of the BCEA, the LRA and related labour legislation, with compliance audits and practical advice for employers in Randburg, Fourways and Johannesburg.

Book a Consultation → Call 011 219 5025

South African employers must comply with a wide range of labour legislation: the Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA), the Labour Relations Act (LRA), the Employment Equity Act (EEA), the National Minimum Wage Act, and the UIF and COIDA requirements, as well as any bargaining council agreement or sectoral determination that covers their industry.

Falling short can lead to labour inspections, compliance orders, fines and disputes at the CCMA. Labour Law Alliance helps businesses understand which rules apply to them, find gaps before they become problems, and put workable fixes in place.

How We Can Help

  • Checks on working hours, overtime, leave and pay against the BCEA and the National Minimum Wage

Frequently Asked Questions

Which labour laws apply to my business?

Almost every employer is covered by the BCEA, the LRA, the EEA, the National Minimum Wage Act, and the UIF and COIDA legislation. Some sectors are also bound by a bargaining council agreement or a sectoral determination, which can set different minimum pay and conditions. We can confirm exactly what applies to you.

How often should we do a compliance audit?

A yearly review is a sensible baseline. It is also worth reviewing whenever legislation changes, your business grows significantly, or you restructure.

What happens if a labour inspector finds non-compliance?

An inspector may issue a compliance order setting out what must be corrected and by when. If it is not complied with, the matter can be taken further, including to the Labour Court. We can help you respond to an inspection and put things right.
